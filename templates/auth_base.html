Bulig

Reports reach adults who can help.

Bulig is the case desk for guidance offices in Philippine schools. Students report without an account; staff follow each case to its close.

  • Students report anonymously. They never need to sign up.
  • Teachers and guidance staff handle cases for their own school.
  • Division offices see counts only, never what a student wrote.

Bulig is Cebuano for "help".
